## 🌟 Overview

**StreamFetch** is a high-speed, privacy-first Android media downloader and offline entertainment player engineered for seamless video streaming, audio ripping, and background playback. Powered by the **OxStream Core™** native engine, it extracts and streams media directly to your device without external proxy delays or intrusive advertising.

Whether you want to save HD tutorials, listen to podcasts while your phone is locked, or manage your offline media library with subfolder organization, StreamFetch provides a clean, fluid experience built on modern **Android Jetpack Compose**.

---

## ⚡ Core Features

### 🚀 1. High-Throughput Stream Pipeline (OxStream Core™)
- **Direct Memory Buffering**: Streams chunks via optimized 64KB high-throughput network pipelines.
- **Adaptive Resolution Support**: Fetch videos in **1080p Full HD**, 720p, 480p, 360p, or pure **MP3 / M4A** high-bitrate audio.
- **No SABR/403 Throttling**: Direct-to-client handshake headers prevent network dropouts and extraction blocks.

### 🎧 2. True Background & Lock-Screen Playback
- **MediaStyle Notification**: Fully responsive Play, Pause, Seek Forward (+10s), and Seek Backward (-10s) right from your lock screen.
- **Background Persistence**: Minimized or screen-off listening continues without being killed by Android battery optimization.
- **Hardware-Accelerated Decoding**: Fluid AV1, H.264, and H.265 video decoding with zero frame dropping.

### 🔍 3. Universal Multi-Language Search
- **Unicode NFD Diacritic-Insensitive Engine**: Search titles in **Bengali (বাংলা)**, Arabic, English, Hindi, and regional scripts without character mismatches.
- **Multi-Token Filtering**: Search simultaneously by title keyword, resolution quality (1080p), or audio format (MP3).

### 🎬 4. Third-Party External Player Support
- Prefer watching in **VLC**, **MX Player**, or **KMPlayer**?
- StreamFetch integrates secure `androidx.core.content.FileProvider` delegation, letting you launch offline media into your favorite player in 1 click.

### 📁 5. Organized Directory Management
- **Tree-View Categorization**: Create custom physical subfolders (e.g., `Tutorials`, `Music`, `Comedy`) directly on storage.
- **Scoped Storage Compliant**: Clean Android 14+ media store routing keeping your phone's internal storage organized.

### 🛡️ 6. 100% Local Device Privacy & Zero Ads
- **Zero Ads**: No banner ads, interstitial popups, or aggressive trackers.
- **Zero Telemetry**: No account required, no phone-home servers, no analytical profiling. All media and histories stay strictly on your local device.
